# Background Music

Keep source metadata for every music track used in the story: title, creator, original download URL, download date, and license/certificate details.

Downloaded Pixabay tracks may be used as part of a new creative work, subject to the Pixabay Content License, but must not be redistributed as standalone audio files. Keep the original stock music files local; do not commit them to this repository. The final story mix may contain the music as one part of the completed creative work.

References:
- [Pixabay Content License Summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)
- [Pixabay Music FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)

Do not add a track to the public project unless its source and license have been checked.
