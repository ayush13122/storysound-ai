# Sound Effects

Keep source metadata for every effect used: title, creator, original download URL, download date, and license/certificate details.

Downloaded Pixabay sound effects may be used as part of a new creative work, subject to the Pixabay Content License, but must not be redistributed as standalone audio files. Keep original stock SFX files local; do not commit them to this repository. The final story mix may contain the effects as part of the completed creative work.

References:
- [Pixabay Content License Summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)
- [Pixabay Terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)

Do not add any sound asset to the public project unless its source and license have been checked.
