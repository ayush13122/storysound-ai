# 2:13 AM — The Last Call

**Language:** English  |  **Genre:** Psychological horror / time-loop mystery  |  **Voices:** Natraj, Rahul, Aravinda

**Production note:** Only dialogue text below is sent to TTS. Scene headings, speaker labels, and SFX directions are not sent to TTS.

## Scene 1 — 2:13 AM

*SFX: Rain outside, ticking clock, phone vibration.*

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

At 2:13 a.m., Aarav was alone in his apartment. Rain rattled the windows, and the hallway light kept flickering. Then his phone rang. The caller ID showed his own number.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

My number? That is impossible.

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Aarav, listen carefully. Do not open the door. There will be three knocks. After that, someone outside will speak in your voice. Do not answer.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

Who is this? How do you know my name?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Because I am you. Five minutes ahead of you. Listen to me, and maybe this time you will survive.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

Aarav wanted to laugh, until the caller described the desk drawer beside him. Behind a broken clock was a small brass key. Aarav opened the drawer. The key was exactly where the stranger said it would be.

## Scene 2 — The key

*SFX: Power cuts; phone torch; three knocks on the door.*

**AARAV (Rahul):**

What does this key open?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

The old bolt at the bottom of your front door. Lock it. Now.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

The lights went out. Using his phone torch, Aarav found the bolt and turned the key. Then came the first knock. A second followed. The third hit so hard that the wooden door shuddered.

**AARAV (Rahul) — outside imitation:**

Aarav! Open up! The voice on the phone is lying. I am trapped out here!

**AARAV (Rahul):**

That is my voice. How can someone outside sound exactly like me?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Do not answer it. It can copy your voice, but it does not know your memories yet. Do not give it anything.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

Aarav scanned the room. Wet footprints crossed the floor, but the window was locked from inside. The prints began at the front door and ended directly in front of the mirror.

## Scene 3 — The reflection

*SFX: Wet footsteps, scratching wood, distant static.*

**AARAV (Rahul):**

The rain is outside. How did wet footprints get into my room?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Because you are looking in the wrong place. The door is only a distraction. Cover the mirror with something. Now.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

The handle slowly turned, but the bolt held. Scratching dragged across the wood, then stopped. Aarav checked his call history. The call had started at 2:18 a.m. His clock still showed 2:13.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

The call is coming from five minutes in the future?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

It happens every time. Last time, I opened the door. Since then, these five minutes have repeated again and again. But this time, you have locked it.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

So it is over?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

No. I kept one thing from you. Whatever is outside does not want to come in. It wants you to recognise it.

## Scene 4 — Five minutes ahead

*SFX: Door handle turns; call-history notification.*

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

Aarav pulled the bedsheet from his bed and stepped toward the mirror. A burst of static cracked through the phone. The voice on the other end suddenly shouted, and for the first time, it sounded terrified.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

Why did you not tell me that before?

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Because it cannot see you until you look at it. Once it recognises your face, it can take your place.

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Stop! If your reflection is already holding the sheet, do not go near it. It is waiting for you.

## Scene 5 — The last reflection

*SFX: Power returns; three soft knocks from the mirror; sudden silence.*

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

The power returned. The hallway was empty. Aarav finally breathed out. Then three soft knocks came from the mirror behind him.

**AARAV (Rahul):**

That sound... it is coming from inside the mirror.

**CALLER (Aravinda):**

Do not look at it. If your reflection smiles, hang up.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

Aarav looked anyway. His reflection stood perfectly still, though his own hands were shaking. Then the reflection smiled. The call disconnected.

**AARAV (Rahul) — reflection:**

Aarav, it is my turn now.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

His phone rang again. The same number appeared, but this time the call was going out from Aarav’s own phone. He refused to answer. Fog spread across the mirror, and words appeared in the glass: See you again at 2:13.

**NARRATOR (Natraj):**

Aarav never opened the door that night. But in the morning, his reflection was gone.

## Voice direction

- Natraj: steady cinematic narration; gradually tighten pacing toward the reveal.
- Rahul: begin confused, then frightened; outside/reflection lines should sound distant and unnervingly familiar.
- Aravinda: low, restrained urgency; keep words clear rather than overacting.

## Mix direction

- Keep rain and room tone below dialogue.
- Use three separated knocks; leave silence after the third.
- Apply telephone filtering to Aravinda only where the caller is on the phone.
- Apply distance/room reverb to Rahul’s outside imitation; retain the same voice identity.
- Use the mirror knocks and final silence as the main scare; do not cover dialogue with music.
- Use original or properly licensed SFX and music; no ElevenLabs music/SFX generation is budgeted.
